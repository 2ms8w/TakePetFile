Privacy Policy

TakePet · Effective November 18, 2026

TakePet (“we”, “us”) is a pet-care companion app. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who receives it, and the choices you have. If you have questions, contact us at supportpetai@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide and that is stored on our servers:

  • Account: your email address and password (the password is handled by our authentication provider and is not readable by us) and your display name.
  • Pet profiles: pet name, species, breed, age, sex, weight, activity level, diet, allergies, medications, medical conditions, vaccination and neutering status, and the pet’s profile photo.
  • Photos: pet photos you choose to add, stored in a private storage area that only your account can access.
  • Subscription status: whether you have TakePet Plus, the product, and its expiry date.
  • Usage counters: a record that you used an AI feature (type and time), so we can apply monthly and daily limits.

Information stored only on your device: health reports, weekly check-in answers, daily care tasks, your AI-consent choice, and your sign-in session. This information is not uploaded to our servers, and it is removed when you delete the app or your account.

Technical information: like most online services, our hosting and authentication providers keep standard server logs (for example IP address and request time) for security and reliability.

We do not use advertising, third-party analytics, or cross-app tracking, and we do not sell your information.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and show you your pets and care information.
  • To provide the AI features you request (chat, photo review, weekly summary).
  • To manage subscriptions and unlock TakePet Plus features.
  • To enforce usage limits, prevent abuse, and keep the service reliable.
  • To respond to support requests and comply with legal obligations.

3. AI features and Google Gemini

When you use AI chat, photo review, or the weekly summary, the content you submit for that request is sent from our servers to Google’s Gemini API to generate a response. Depending on the feature, this can include your message, the conversation so far, the pet details relevant to the request (for example species, breed, age, weight, allergies, medications, medical conditions), the photo you selected, and your check-in answers.

Before you first use an AI feature, TakePet asks for your permission. If you decline, the AI features are not used and nothing is sent to Google. We do not store your chat messages or scan photos on our servers for the purpose of AI; our servers keep only the usage counter described above. Google processes the content under its own terms and privacy policy.

AI responses are general educational information. They are not a veterinary diagnosis, treatment, or emergency service. Contact a veterinarian for any health concern, and immediately for urgent symptoms.

4. Who receives information

  • Supabase — authentication, database, file storage and server functions. Our project is hosted in the EU (Stockholm).
  • Google (Gemini API) — processes AI requests, as described above.
  • RevenueCat — receives your account identifier and purchase information to verify and manage subscriptions.
  • Apple — processes payments and subscriptions through the App Store. We never receive your payment card details.

These providers act on our behalf or under their own terms. We do not share your information with anyone else, except when required by law.

5. International processing

Our database and files are stored in the EU. Google, RevenueCat and Apple may process data in other countries, including the United States, under their own safeguards.

6. Retention and deletion

We keep your information while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time in the app: Profile → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, profile, pet profiles, pet photos, usage records and subscription record from our systems. Copies may remain in provider backups for a limited period before being overwritten.

Deleting your account does not cancel an Apple subscription. Cancel it first in iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. Apple and RevenueCat may keep purchase records as required by their policies and by tax and accounting law.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under Moroccan Law 09-08 and the EU GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, and to receive a copy of it. You can view and edit most information in the app. For anything else, write to supportpetai@gmail.com; we aim to reply within 30 days. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

8. Security

We use encrypted connections, per-user access rules on our database and file storage, and server-side checks for every AI and subscription request. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Children

TakePet is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where local law requires). We do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version, and we will notify you in the app for significant changes.
